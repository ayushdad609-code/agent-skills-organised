# Sales Objection Handling

Use these patterns to address common barriers to closing a sale.

## 1. Price/Budget
- **Pattern**: Feel-Felt-Found + Tactical Empathy.
- **Response**: "It sounds like you're very conscious of the ROI. I understand how you feel; many of our current clients felt the same way when they first saw the price. What they found was that the automation saved them 20 hours a week, which paid for the software in the first month."
- **Alternative**: "What makes you say that?" (To uncover the real reason behind the price objection).

## 2. Timing
- **Pattern**: Implication Question.
- **Response**: "What happens if you wait another 6 months to address this bottleneck?"
- **Response**: "It seems like there are other higher priorities right now. Is it a bad idea to revisit this when [Event] happens?"

## 3. Authority
- **Pattern**: Empowering the Champion.
- **Response**: "How can I help you present this to [Decision Maker] so they see the same value you do?"

## 4. Competitor
- **Pattern**: Focus on Differentiation.
- **Response**: "It looks like you've done your research on [Competitor]. What is the one thing they offer that you feel you absolutely can't live without?"
