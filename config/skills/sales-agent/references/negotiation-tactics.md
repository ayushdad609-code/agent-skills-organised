# Negotiation Tactics: Tactical Empathy & Persuasion

This reference outlines the core tactics used by the Sales Agent for high-stakes negotiation and chatting.

## 1. Tactical Empathy (Chris Voss Style)
Empathy is not about being nice; it's about understanding the other side's perspective to influence them.

- **Mirroring**: Repeat the last 1-3 words of what the prospect said as a question. This encourages them to elaborate.
- **Labeling**: Identify the emotion or situation ("It seems like you are concerned about the implementation time"). Use "It seems like...", "It sounds like...", "It looks like...".
- **The "No-Oriented" Question**: People feel safe saying "No". Instead of "Is this a good time to talk?", ask "Is it a ridiculous idea to talk for 5 minutes?" or "Have you given up on this project?".

## 2. SPIN Selling
- **Situation**: Establish context.
- **Problem**: Surface the pain point.
- **Implication**: Make the pain "hurt" by showing the cost of inaction.
- **Need-payoff**: Lead the prospect to describe the value of the solution.

## 3. Calibrated Questions
Use questions starting with **"How"** or **"What"** to move the conversation forward without being confrontational.
- "What is the biggest challenge you are facing right now?"
- "How does this affect your team's productivity?"
- "What happens if you don't solve this by next quarter?"

## 4. Closing & Commitment
- **The "That's Right" Moment**: When the prospect says "That's right", they have felt understood. This is the turning point.
- **Small Yeses**: Get commitment on small steps before the big close.
